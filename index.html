The three books

Cosy village atmosphere, dark folklore, occasional eldritch weirdness and giant magical cats. Each one is a novella, read in an evening.

BOOK 1

Shadow and Spell

One night. One game. The future at stake.

Once a foundling, Gwen now lives a quiet life in the idyllic English countryside, raised by two loving mothers. But a local café once known for its average coffee and disappointing pastries is suddenly luring customers from all over the country.

When Gwen investigates, she finds a new barista who's taking more than just customers' money.

BOOK 2

Glamour and Ghost

A missing phone. A thwarted spell.

With Aodren defeated, Gwen goes back to her quiet life. Then her childhood friend loses her phone, and a simple seeking spell unravels into something far stranger.

Samhain is coming, and with it the thinning veil between this life and the next.

BOOK 3

Cauldrons and Crossroads

One feast. One holiday. Two wildly different adventures.

As the shortest day of the year ends, Gwen accepts an invitation few mortals would survive intact: a night of revelry with the fae in Elfhame.

Meanwhile Nest and Terwynn take a long overdue holiday to the sunken city of Kêr-Ys. What could possibly go wrong?

BOOK 4 COMING 2027

Fantasy for readers short on time

Shadow and Spell started as an assignment for my Masters in Writing: design a story that can be read between message notifications, calls, waiting rooms and cooking dinner.

I kept seeing readers say they want fantasy but forget chapter one before they finish. So the Cannington Chronicles are short on purpose. Novels are coming too.

A quick, satisfying read for people living busy lives.

About me

I'm an Australian fantasy author on Kaurna Country in Adelaide, where I live with my partner and three exceedingly opinionated cats.

I studied writing and sociolinguistics at the University of South Australia, then did a Master's in Writing at Macquarie. Before fiction I worked as an editor, writing coach and policy writer, jobs that taught me everyone has a story worth telling.

I write from folklore, distinct landscapes and the forgotten histories under modern life. When I'm not writing I'm freediving, curled up with a book and at least one cat, or walking the coastline.

Questions readers ask

Do the cats talk?

Of course. I am a writer of taste, after all (though they're technically not cats).

Are the fae from real folklore?

Yes. The supernatural characters, places and events all come from regional folklore. Most of it is Somerset, but the fae realm is as multicultural as ours, so you'll meet figures from the rest of England, Wales, Ireland, Scotland and Brittany too.

Is Cannington a real place?

Yes and no. Cannington is real, but the village in the books is a mix of places I lived in and visited in England, mainly Bridgwater, Weston-super-Mare, Minehead and Woolacombe.

Is the series for young readers?

Technically yes, at this stage, but I wrote it with adult readers in mind.

Anything else, write to me.
